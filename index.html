Who we are

A cooperative built by staff, for staff.

Every contribution is part of a shared pool designed to improve member well-being and create practical financial value.

Registered as a Cooperative Multipurpose Society in 1993 with two hundred pioneering members, WEMACOOP has grown around savings, loans, shares and property acquisition for Wema Bank staff.
